Projects

Featured build

Projectile Motion Simulator

A Python physics engine that models projectile motion on Earth, the Moon, or Mars — computing max height, range, and flight time from launch angle and speed. It goes beyond the closed-form equations too: an Euler-integration mode adds air resistance (drag proportional to velocity squared), since drag can't be solved in closed form, and a comparison mode overlays two trajectories — like ideal vs. drag-affected flight — side by side. Results are visualized as animated trajectory, velocity-time, and height-time plots, with simulation data exportable to CSV.
